# RoomDrop

## What This Is

A mobile-first web app where you upload a photo of your room, then drop in cushions, furniture, and wall art from your own product images to see how they'd look. Backgrounds are removed automatically so items sit naturally in the scene. Aimed at friends, family, and as a portfolio piece — not a commercial product.

## Core Value

You can take a photo of your real room and convincingly preview how a product you're considering buying would look in it — without leaving the browser and without it costing anything to run.

### Out of Scope

<!-- Explicit boundaries. Includes reasoning to prevent re-adding. -->

- Curated/affiliate product catalog — users bring their own product images; no commerce
- Accounts and cloud sync — local-only persistence keeps hosting at $0
- Shareable URLs / multi-device sync — requires backend storage, deferred until there's demand
- Surface-aware placement (snap to floor/wall, perspective warping) — too complex for v1; manual placement is good enough for a portfolio piece
- Server-side background removal — would consume free-tier compute and add a backend; in-browser keeps cost at $0
- Monetization (ads, affiliate, paid tier) — not the point of v1
- Desktop-only optimizations — the photo-of-your-room flow is inherently a phone-first moment

## Context

- **Prototype already exists** under `poc/` — a Claude.ai dc-artifact (`Cushion Stylist.dc.html`) that demonstrates the cushion drag-and-drop flow against a single uploaded room photo. It is a UX reference, not a starting point: `poc/support.js` is generated by an external `dc-runtime` project and only runs inside the Claude.ai artifact host (relies on injected `window.React` / `window.ReactDOM`).
- The prototype covers cushions only; furniture and wall art are unimplemented; background removal is unimplemented; nothing persists.
- The codebase map (`.planning/codebase/`) treats the project as effectively greenfield — stack, build tooling, framework choice are all open.
- The product moment is mobile: people photograph their room on their phone, then want to drop a thing onto it. Desktop is secondary.
- Background removal quality is the main perceptual driver. If items look pasted in, the illusion breaks and the app feels like a toy.

## Constraints

- **Budget**: Must run on the DanubeData free tier (100 MB storage, 10 GB/mo bandwidth) indefinitely — no monthly bill for hosting, storage, or inference. Vercel Hobby was rejected because its ToS forbids commercial use and we may monetize later.
- **Architecture**: Client-only. No backend, no database, no server-side ML. All compute happens in the user's browser.
- **Persistence**: `localStorage` only. No accounts, no cloud, no cross-device sync.
- **Device target**: Mobile-first responsive. Must work on a mid-range phone — including the background-removal step.
- **Catalog**: Bring-your-own product images. No product database, no scraping, no affiliate plumbing in v1.
- **Background removal**: Must run fully client-side, with a user-selectable quality/speed tradeoff to accommodate weaker devices.
- **Reference UX**: The prototype's upload → drag → place → reset flow is the spec for cushions. Furniture and wall art extend the same model (free move/scale/rotate, no surface snapping).

## Key Decisions

| Decision | Rationale | Outcome |
|----------|-----------|---------|
| Client-only architecture, no backend | Keeps hosting cost at $0 and matches the portfolio-scale audience | — Pending |
| In-browser background removal | Cost constraint forbids server-side inference; quality matters too much to skip it | — Pending |
| User-selectable BG removal quality (fast vs quality) | Reconciles "mobile-first" with the size/perf cost of strong segmentation models | — Pending |
| `localStorage` persistence, no accounts | Avoids backend + auth scope; acceptable for friends/portfolio use | — Pending |
| Bring-your-own product images | Removes catalog, scraping, licensing, and commerce scope from v1 | — Pending |
| Manual placement (no surface snapping or perspective warping) | Scene-aware placement is a research project; manual is good enough for v1 and mobile | — Pending |
| Discard the dc-runtime prototype as a code base | `poc/support.js` is generated and only runs in the Claude.ai artifact host; keep `poc/` as UX reference only | — Pending |
| Static deploy to DanubeData free tier | Matches client-only architecture and $0 budget; preserves option to monetize later (Vercel Hobby ToS forbids commercial use); EU residency / GDPR-by-default; tradeoff is a 10 GB/mo bandwidth cap, acceptable at portfolio scale | — Pending |
| Use `@imgly/background-removal` (AGPL-3.0) for v1; defer license decision | AGPL requires the deployed app's source to be public; compatible with affiliate/ads monetization but blocks closed-source paid tiers.
