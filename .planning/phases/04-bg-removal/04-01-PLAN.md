# Plan 04-01: In-Browser Background Removal (single plan)

**Phase:** 4 — In-Browser Background Removal
**Status:** planned
**Requirements:** BGR-01..06

## Tasks
1. **settingsStore** — persisted `bgMode: 'fast'|'quality'|null` (null = probe default); WebGPU probe helper (cached promise).
2. **bgProgressStore** — transient per-item progress (module store, useSyncExternalStore).
3. **bgRemovalService** — sequential queue, job registry with cancel (fetch abort + result discard), progress wiring, first-download toast, success (cutout blob swap + old blob GC), failure (status+reason), re-run.
4. **UI** — LibraryThumb status badge w/ percent, cancel-while-processing, re-run button; SettingsMenu gear popover in header.
5. **Wiring** — auto-enqueue in `uploadLibraryItems`; vite `optimizeDeps.exclude` for the package.
6. **Tests** — queue/cancel semantics with a mocked `removeBackground`.
